Billing FAQ

Good to know.

How does the free trial work?

Tell us about your business and we'll set up your workspace with the plan you choose. You get full access for 14 days. No card is needed to start, and nothing is charged unless you decide to continue.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Upgrade at any time and the new modules switch on immediately with all your existing data. Downgrades take effect from your next billing period.

How do I pay?

We invoice monthly or yearly in PKR or USD. You can pay by bank transfer or card. Yearly billing gives you two months free.

What counts as a user?

Anyone who signs in to your workspace — owners, managers, salespeople, accountants or delivery staff. Customers and vendors never count as users.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

Your data belongs to you. Before your subscription ends you can export your records, and we keep your workspace read-only for 30 days in case you change your mind.
