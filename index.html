How it works

Move an order forward. Everything else updates itself.

Each step of an order triggers the right updates to stock, cash, books and customer messages — so your team does the selling and SmartSell does the paperwork.

Capture the sale

Pick the customer by phone number, add products and discounts, assign the salesperson and take an advance if the customer pays upfront.

  • Customer found or createdLocal and international phone formats match the same person.
  • Advance recordedBooked as a customer advance — not revenue — until delivery.
  • Prices locked inLater catalogue changes never rewrite this order.

Reserve the stock

Confirming the order sets aside inventory at the selected branch so it can't be sold twice or transferred away.

  • Inventory reservedAvailable stock = on hand minus reserved, at every location.
  • Confirmation sent on WhatsAppCustomers with consent get an instant order confirmation.
  • Low-stock warningsItems dropping below reorder level appear on the dashboard.

Prepare for hand-over

Mark the order ready for delivery or pickup. Pickup orders skip straight to delivered when the customer collects.

  • Delivery scheduledUpcoming deliveries are listed with their due dates.
  • "Your order is ready" messageSent automatically, in your own wording.
  • Balance due visibleStaff know exactly what to collect before it leaves.

On the road

Dispatch the order and keep the customer informed. Late deliveries are flagged as exceptions for follow-up.

  • Out-for-delivery updateThe customer knows it's on the way.
  • Exceptions surfacedOverdue deliveries go to the top of today's priorities.
  • Full status historyWho moved the order, and when.

Close the books — automatically

Delivery turns the order into a recognised sale. Stock, revenue, costs, commissions and vendor shares are all posted in one step.

  • Sale & journal postedRevenue, cost of goods, receivables and advances balanced.
  • Commission earnedCalculated on merchandise after discounts, excluding delivery fees.
  • Thank-you & remindersUnpaid balances get polite, scheduled payment reminders.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need to know accounting to use SmartSell?

No. You record everyday events — orders, deliveries, payments and expenses — and SmartSell writes the balanced accounting entries for you. Your accountant gets a clean journal and profit & loss whenever they need it.

Can I manage more than one branch or warehouse?

Yes. Stock is tracked per location, you can transfer between branches, and dashboards and reports can be filtered by location. The number of locations depends on your plan.

How does the WhatsApp integration work?

The quickest way is to scan a QR code from WhatsApp → Linked devices, just like WhatsApp Web. Businesses sending at higher volumes can connect the official WhatsApp Business Cloud API instead. Either way, messages only go to customers who have opted in.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. SmartSell is fully responsive and can be installed on Android, iPhone, Windows or Mac directly from the browser, so your team can take orders and update deliveries from anywhere.

Can you move my existing data into SmartSell?

Yes. We can import your products, customers, vendors and opening stock from spreadsheets as part of onboarding. Larger migrations are available as a one-time service.
